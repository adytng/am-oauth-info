AgentMoney 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-18

誰在使用 / Who uses it

AgentMoney 只有一位使用者，就是它的擁有者本人。它不對其他人提供服務，也不蒐集任何其他人的資料。

AgentMoney has a single user: its owner. It serves no one else and collects no data about anyone else.

存取哪些資料 / What data is accessed

經擁有者授權後，AgentMoney 會存取擁有者自己的 Google 帳戶中的下列資料：Gmail 的帳單與交易通知信、Google 雲端硬碟中的對帳單檔案、Google 試算表中的匯率與行情。

With the owner's authorization, AgentMoney accesses the following data in the owner's own Google account: statement and transaction notice mails in Gmail, statement files in Google Drive, and exchange rates and prices in Google Sheets.

如何使用 / How the data is used

這些資料只用來替擁有者記帳、對帳，以及產生寄給擁有者本人的每日彙報。

The data is used only to keep and reconcile the owner's books and to produce a daily report that is sent to the owner.

儲存與分享 / Storage and sharing

資料儲存在擁有者自己管理的設備上。AgentMoney 不會把從 Google 取得的資料出售、轉讓或分享給任何第三方，也不會用於廣告。

The data is stored on equipment managed by the owner. AgentMoney does not sell, transfer or share data obtained from Google with any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

AgentMoney 對從 Google API 取得的資訊的使用與移轉，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包含其中的「有限使用」規定。

AgentMoney's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

撤銷授權 / Revoking access

擁有者可以隨時在 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權設定撤銷授權；撤銷後 AgentMoney 就無法再存取上述資料。

The owner can revoke access at any time in the third-party access settings of the Google account; after that AgentMoney can no longer access the data above.
